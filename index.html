Signal, not noise

I'm a first-year undergraduate at IIT Bhilai pursuing a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering. My work sits at the overlap of three fields: the low-level precision of electrical systems, the pattern-hunting of quantitative finance, and the craft of building things people actually use on the web.
